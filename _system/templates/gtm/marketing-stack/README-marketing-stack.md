This is the shared marketing-tool contract for every company and personal brand in this Vault. It belongs to the reusable GTM template, not to one teamspace or to general Vault documentation. Do not copy it into a teamspace.

Use the owning application or infrastructure docs for an actual service's configuration and account status. Do not maintain a second live tool inventory here. Never store credentials, API keys, or tokens in marketing notes.

- Capture and CRM: keep person and company identity, status, and follow-up in the chosen operational CRM. Treat imports and newsletter signups as sources until their records are reconciled. [[README-crm]] documents the current Vault note workflow for individual capture.
- Email and newsletters: the sending platform owns subscriptions, consent, delivery, and engagement state. A relationship note may link to it, but does not replace it.
- Publishing and analytics: the publishing service owns scheduled and published state; analytics systems own measured events. Campaign notes in the teamspace hold intent, links, results, and interpretation.
- Automation: integrations move data between owners. They must not silently make a copied list or another teamspace's CRM authoritative.

Keep positioning, campaigns, offers, funnels, and relationship memory in each owning teamspace. Shared tools and overlapping audiences do not merge the two companies' GTM plans or CRMs.
