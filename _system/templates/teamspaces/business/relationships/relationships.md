One record per person, company, or qualified opportunity.

Open [[relationship-crm.base]] for the shared pipeline and follow-up views.

- `people/`: durable person records
- `companies/`: durable company records
- `opportunities/`: qualified commercial pursuits and proposals
- `imports/`: untouched CSV and workbook source files

Keep bulk prospect lists in `imports/`. Create a person or company note when the
relationship is worth curating. Create an opportunity only after a need or
commercial path is qualified.

Use email as the preferred unique identifier for a person and domain for a
company. Those identifiers also make a later Attio import safer.
