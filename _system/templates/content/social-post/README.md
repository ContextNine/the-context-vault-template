This is the single editable social-post template. Bootstrap renders `content-item-template.md` into the shared Obsidian template at `_system/_obsidian/templates/shared/content/`, replacing `{{managed_properties}}` with the managed-file fields.

Social content is an independent teamspace capability. It is not part of the business or personal-brand physical packs. Published and draft posts belong under the selected teamspace's `_obsidian/content/items/social-posts/`; the source template stays here.
